Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is delivery?

Most top-ups are delivered automatically within seconds to a few minutes after payment is verified.

How do I add funds to my wallet?

Go to Dashboard → Wallet → Deposit Funds, choose a payment method, and submit your reference/proof. Your balance updates once an admin verifies the payment.

What if my order fails?

Failed orders are automatically refunded to your wallet. You can also open a support ticket from your dashboard.

Terms of Service

By using Supper Shop you agree to purchase digital goods for personal use only, provide accurate player/account IDs, and understand that delivered digital currency cannot be reversed once fulfilled by the game publisher.

Privacy Policy

We store only the account information required to process orders and support requests. Payment proof images are stored securely and accessible only to you and authorized admins.

Refund Policy

Orders that fail to deliver are refunded to your wallet automatically. Refund requests for delivered orders are reviewed case by case — open a ticket with your order ID.
